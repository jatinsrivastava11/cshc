[ 02 ] HOW IT WORKS

Every job runs the same way

Same rhythm every time: show up, learn the concept, get the piece you need, go pull off the heist.

STEP_01
Sign in. Your crew's current job is waiting — locked until you're cleared for it.
STEP_02
Come to the meeting. We teach that week's concept and hand out the piece the website doesn't have — sometimes a phrase, sometimes a wordlist, sometimes a physical prop.
STEP_03
Crack it — solo or with your crew, on your own time, before the next meeting.
STEP_04
Stuck? Hints are on the site — each one costs points off your haul. Whoever clears the final job first wins.
